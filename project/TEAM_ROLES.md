# Suggested Team Roles

Recommended team size: **3–5 members**.

## Data Preparation Scientist / Engineer
Owns:
- source acquisition;
- joins;
- incident-level aggregation;
- data quality.

## Feature Engineering & Leakage Lead
Owns:
- feature design;
- availability assessment;
- leakage audit;
- preprocessing.

## Classification Data Scientist
Owns:
- baseline;
- candidate models;
- training;
- class imbalance handling.

## Model Evaluation & Explainability Lead
Owns:
- metrics;
- threshold analysis;
- calibration;
- SHAP/explainability;
- error analysis.

## Data Product / Technical Lead
May be combined with another role.

Owns:
- problem framing;
- operational interpretation;
- integration;
- reproducibility;
- documentation;
- final QA.

Every team member must make substantive technical contributions.
